\section{Question}
Can an LLM stand in for a
population of minds?

\section{Setup}
\begin{itemize}
\item $N=277$ PhilPeople philosophers
\item 100 PhilPapers 2020 questions
\item 7 profile-conditioned LLMs
\item Temperature 0; Accept $\to$ Reject
coded onto $[0,1]$
\item Checked against PhilPapers 2020
($N=1{,}785$)
\end{itemize}
